**Airbnb Market Intelligence — NYC Pricing & Rating Analysis (R + ggplot2)**

Explored 30,000+ New York City Airbnb listings to understand how neighborhood, room type, host quality, and guest experience influence nightly pricing.
Using R (tidyverse) and ggplot2, this project identifies the drivers behind price variation and provides insights that can guide host strategy and market positioning.

**📌 Project Overview**

This analysis examines Airbnb listings across New York City with the goal of uncovering pricing patterns, rating relationships, and market characteristics that influence host performance.

All data cleaning, wrangling, and visualization was completed in R, and the final analytical report was produced using R Markdown.

**❓ Business Questions**

* This project addresses key market intelligence questions:
* Which NYC neighborhoods are the most expensive, and why?
* How does room type affect nightly price?
* Do higher-rated listings command higher prices?
* What is the typical price range after removing extreme outliers?
* What factors may influence guest perception and pricing power?

**📊 Key Insights**

1. Neighborhood Price Differences
Manhattan is the highest-priced borough, followed by Brooklyn.
Price patterns align with tourism density and proximity to major attractions.

2. Room Type Price Impact
Entire homes/apartments cost 2–3× more than private or shared rooms.
Room type is one of the strongest predictors of nightly price.

3. Ratings vs. Price
Highly rated listings tend to have slightly higher prices.
Suggests host quality and guest satisfaction influence perceived value.

4. Price Distribution (Cleaned)
After removing extreme outliers, NYC market prices cluster between $80–$250 per night.
Majority of listings fall within this realistic operating range.
